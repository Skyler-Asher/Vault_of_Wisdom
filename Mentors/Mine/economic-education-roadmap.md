# The Economic Education Ladder
### A step-by-step roadmap through your 38 videos

Each tier builds on the one before it. Don't skip ahead — Tier 4 (macro) won't fully click until Tier 1–3 (the basic vocabulary) is solid.

---

## 🪜 Tier 1 — The Bedrock: "What is money, actually?"
*Goal: understand the raw materials before touching strategy.*

1. **Why Paper Money Has Value? Fiat Money Explained** — gold standard → fiat, and why trust is the whole game.
2. **How Banks Create Money From Nothing (Fractional Reserve Banking)** — deposits, loans, and "loans create deposits."
3. **GDP Fully Explained: Per Capita, PPP, Nominal** — how we measure the size of an economy.
4. **What are Stocks? And How do They Work?** — ownership, shares, why companies issue them.

---

## 🪜 Tier 2 — Personal Finance Survival Kit
*Goal: don't lose money before you learn to grow it.*

5. **The Fastest Way To Get Poor** — the 10 habits that sink people financially.
6. **Credit Cards EXPLAINED: The Traps, Rewards, and Hidden Truths** — minimum payments, interest traps.
7. **7 Levels of Wealth They Never Taught You** — the ladder you're on, from survival to abundance.
8. **Why the Rich Pretend to Be Poor (And Why You Should Too)** — stealth wealth, avoiding lifestyle inflation.

---

## 🪜 Tier 3 — Investing Fundamentals
*Goal: move from saving to actually building assets.*

9. **The Difference Between Trading and Investing** — short-term charts vs long-term fundamentals.

11. **Stock Market Crash? Don't Panic, Get Rich Instead** — dollar-cost averaging, cycles.

### How to use this
- **Pace:** 2–3 videos a week keeps concepts from blurring together; this is a ~4-month ladder if you go steady.
- **Test yourself between tiers:** before moving to Tier 4, try explaining Tiers 1–3 out loud to someone else — if you can't, rewatch rather than push forward.
- **Tier 5–7 are non-linear** — once you finish Tier 4, you can reorder 5/6/7 based on what interests you most (wealth strategy vs. crime vs. geopolitics all build on the same Tier 1–4 base).
